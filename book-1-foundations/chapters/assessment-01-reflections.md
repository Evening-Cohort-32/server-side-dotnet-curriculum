# Brass & Poem: Self-Reflection :thinking:

## What this is

You've passed the automated tests for Brass & Poem. Nice work. Before your 1:1
review with an instructor, work through the questions below on your own and write
down real answers, not just a checkmark. Try to explain each one out loud or in
writing, the way you would to someone who's never seen the code.

This is individual. There's no team to review it with here. Be honest with
yourself about where you're solid and where you're not; that's the whole point.

Some of these questions are ones your instructor will likely ask you directly,
using your own Brass & Poem code as the starting point. Others aren't something
your code naturally gives an instructor a reason to bring up, so this list is your
only real check on those. Don't skip them just because no one's going to quiz
you on them.

- 🗣️ **We'll probably talk about this together.** Your Brass & Poem code gives a
  natural, concrete way to get into it.
- 📓 **Self-check only.** Nothing in Brass & Poem forces this to come up, so this
  is on you. Ask your instructor if anything here is unclear.

## Console I/O & Strings

- 🗣️ Can I explain what `Console.ReadLine()` returns, and why you almost always
  have to convert what it gives you before using it?
- 🗣️ Can I explain what a verbatim string (`@"..."`) is for, and how it behaves
  differently from a regular string?
- 📓 Am I comfortable building output with string interpolation (`$"..."`)?

## Conditionals & Loops

- 🗣️ Can I explain the difference between an `if`/`else` and a ternary
  expression, and when I'd reach for each?
- 🗣️ Can I explain what a `foreach` loop is doing differently from a regular
  `for` loop?
- 🗣️ Can I explain what a `switch` statement's `default` case (or an `if`
  chain's final `else`) is for?
- 📓 Do I understand the difference between `while` and `do...while`?

## Working with Numbers

- 🗣️ Can I explain the difference between `int`, `double`, and `decimal`, and
  say why you'd pick one over another?
- 🗣️ Can I explain what a cast is and give an example?
- 🗣️ Can I explain what "parsing" means, and how it's different from casting?
- 🗣️ Can I explain what happens if you try to parse text that isn't a valid
  number?

## Collections: Lists, Arrays, Dictionaries

- 🗣️ Can I explain what a `List<T>` is, and how it's different from an array?
- 🗣️ Can I name at least two `List` methods and what each one does (e.g. `Add`,
  `Remove`, `RemoveAt`)?
- 📓 Can I explain what a `Dictionary` is and when I'd reach for one instead of
  a `List`?
- 📓 Do I understand when I'd choose an array over a `List`?

## Classes & Object-Oriented Basics

- 🗣️ Can I explain what a class is, in my own words?
- 🗣️ Can I explain the difference between a property and a plain variable?
- 🗣️ Can I explain what `get` and `set` are each doing on a property?
- 🗣️ Can I explain what `new` does, and what an object initializer
  (`new SomeClass { ... }`) is doing?
- 📓 Can I explain what "abstraction" means as it relates to writing a class?
- 📓 Do I understand what access modifiers like `public` and `private` control?

## DateTime

- 📓 Can I create a `DateTime` and pull parts out of it (like the day of the
  week, or just the date)?
- 📓 Can I explain the difference between `DateTime.Now` and a `DateTime` I
  construct myself?

## Exceptions & Error Handling

- 📓 Can I explain the difference between a compiler error and a runtime
  exception?
- 📓 Can I explain what `try`/`catch` does and when I'd reach for it?
- 📓 Can I name a built-in exception type and what typically causes it?

## LINQ

- 🗣️ Can I explain what `First` does, and what happens if nothing matches?
- 🗣️ Can I explain the difference between `First` and `FirstOrDefault`?
- 🗣️ Can I explain what `Select` does, in my own words?
- 🗣️ Can I explain what `Where` does, and how it's different from `Select`?
- 🗣️ Can I write a lambda expression (`x => ...`) and explain what each part of
  it means?

## Methods

- 🗣️ Can I explain what a parameter is, and how it's different from a return
  value?
- 🗣️ Can I explain what `void` means as a return type?
- 🗣️ Can I explain what a local function is, and how it's different from a
  method that belongs to a class?
